# BRIEF · KasirKita Modul 01 (Scroll-Craft Edition)

*Creative direction: Self-authored under explicit user delegation (User selected Opsi 2 for /scroll-craft prototype).*

---

## 1. Vibe & Core Identity
- **Vibe:** Swiss Technical Precision, Cinematic Spatial Calm, Architectural Clarity, Non-Neon Dark/Light Elegance.
- **Medium References:**
  1. *Braun / Dieter Rams Design Ethos:* Clean hairlines, utilitarian beauty, zero ornamental clutter.
  2. *Linear.app & Apple Hardware Keynotes:* Tactile spatial depth, smooth scroll pacing, crisp typography.
  3. *Technical Blueprints / Architectural Drafts:* High contrast line-weights, monochrome grid, discrete callout pins.

---

## 2. The Visitor Journey (Beat by Beat)
1. **Recognition (Act 1 · `scrub`):** The visitor sees the cinematic 3D abstract form scrubbing under their wheel with a clear, authoritative promise: *"Siapkan Dokumen Sebelum Membuat Program."*
2. **Tension (Act 2 · `pin`):** The frame locks while the cost of reckless coding is revealed line-by-line: jumping into C code without SDLC leads to broken structs, duplicate files, and corrupted transactions.
3. **The Turn (Act 3 · `pin` + Signature Move · PEAK):** The core breakthrough of KasirKita: *Pemisahan Master vs Transaksi*. An SVG technical blueprint builds its connection live as the user scrolls, showing Primary Key $\to$ Foreign Key linkage and stock decrementing in real-time.
4. **Substance (Act 4 · `flow`):** The 9 structured chapters of Modul 01, from SDLC workflow to Activity Plan, presented in high-legibility cards with collapsible technical details.
5. **Commitment (Act 5 · `pin` + `spotlight`):** A live terminal simulation of KasirKita validating preparation docs, culminating in the next action: Modul 02 or Daftar Modul.

---

## 3. The Energy & Feeling Curve
- **Act 1 (Hero):** *Calm Authority & Awe.* The smooth video scrubs gently under the hand.
- **Act 2 (Tension):** *Focus & Realization.* The user pauses to understand the architectural risks.
- **Act 3 (The Turn - PEAK):** *Illumination & Delight.* The one moment they remember: watching the data flow between Master and Transaction live on the blueprint.
- **Act 4 (Curriculum):** *Substantial Clarity.* Steady, comfortable reading pace with zero eye fatigue.
- **Act 5 (Close):** *Resolved Confidence.* Clean closure with interactive terminal feedback.

## 4. The Peak
- **The Peak Moment:** *Act 3 (Fig 1.6 Cetak Biru Interaktif).*
- **Tell-Someone Sentence:** *"Ini website tutorial yang waktu di-scroll, diagram relasi datanya menggambar jalurnya sendiri dari master ke nota belanja dan stoknya langsung berkurang."*

## 5. The Signature Move
- **Bespoke Interaction:** *Live Blueprint Stroke-Build & Stock Flux.*
  - Driven by act progress (`--sc-p`) without touching the engine:
  - SVG connection lines dynamically draw themselves via `stroke-dashoffset`.
  - Number indicators for Primary Key, Foreign Key, and Stock Mutation animate into focus sequentially.
  - The stock count dynamically animates from `20` to `18 (-2)` at the exact midpoint of the act.
